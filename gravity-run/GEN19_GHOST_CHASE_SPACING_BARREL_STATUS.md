## Historical follow-up findings (superseded where noted above)

- The current Gen19 model is variant 3 (`warning=90`, `danger=200`, `pursuit_start_lag=330`, `pursuit_speed_delta=220`), not the older stationary-warning variant 1 used in an earlier lag estimate. Its model positions the ghost to catch up during warning and overtake during the danger window. The actual SP capture described above confirms this controlled encounter, but its scripted 12-tick flip is not a human-reaction study.
- A matched attempt to lower the pursuit gap from 4000 to 3500px passed the selected routes and kept the synthetic gap boundary, but did not change the cohort's 13 accepted pursuits. The 4000px value is restored. Increasing density to 2.6 is outside `CourseGenerationRuleset.event_density`'s supported maximum of 2.5; it was immediately reverted without changing course identity. The more aggressive 10% spacing-scale trial also remains reverted after 3/20 invalid manifests.
- The barrel attribution maps causes at destruction poses; the reach rows are per-speed floor-path outcomes. Both remain bounded to four seeds and do not cover ceiling-switch routes or establish every loss as user-visible. The earlier comparison counts in this historical section were produced by a different run and are withdrawn; only the matched dataset in the current checkpoint should be used.
- Existing barrel destruction/ledger policy is unchanged. At canonical 500px/s the current candidate's floor-path reach fraction is lower than Gen18 in this sample, so the reported barrel issue remains unresolved. The cohort's worst-gap reduction is modest and does not establish that generated courses generally contain shorter quiet periods.
